WT Reticle Studio Pro: Quick Guide & War Thunder Setup Read README.md for full documentation

1. Example Sights & Auto-Discovery in sights/

The sights/ folder includes ready-to-use example sights and acts as a live library. Drop any .blk file into sights/ and run Start-ReticleStudio.bat (or click 📁 Link sights/ in the top bar) to auto-discover every sight in the dropdown.

2. Drawing Over Existing Sights (Even Dynamic/HUD Sights)

Select any sight from sights/ or click Overlay .blk. Dynamic HUD presets automatically have their static crosshair & range scale synthesized into the Base Layer. Lock the Base Layer (🔒) and draw your custom artwork on the Drawn Art Overlay layer!

3. Auto-Drawing Any Reference Image

Click Ref Image (or drag & drop a PNG/JPG/WebP onto the canvas). In Ref Image & Pro Auto-Draw, choose Smart Line Art for clean single-stroke outlines & exact eye/shape borders, or Hybrid Art for lines + filled quads. Adjust Sensitivity while watching the cyan live preview, then click Auto-Draw Image → Reticle.

4. Optimizing Sight Complexity

Use the Detail Kept (5%–99%) slider in Sight Optimizer & Complexity to smoothly reduce line/quad counts while staying inside War Thunder's engine limits. Click ↩ Undo Optimize anytime to restore full detail.

5. Installing Your Sight & Troubleshooting

Click Save .blk (make sure the sight filename has no spaces, e.g. my_sight.blk) and place it inside:
• Specific vehicle: C:\Users\<username>\Documents\My Games\WarThunder\Saves\<player-ID>\production\UserSights\<tank-ID>\<sight-name>.blk
• All ground vehicles: C:\Users\<username>\Documents\My Games\WarThunder\Saves\<player-ID>\production\UserSights\all_tanks\<sight-name>.blk
Troubleshooting: Alt + F9 to reload sights in-game most probably will not work (a long-standing War Thunder bug). Reboot the game after adding or editing a sight, and ensure the sight filename contains no spaces.
