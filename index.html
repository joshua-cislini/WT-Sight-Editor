WT Reticle Studio Pro: Quick Guide & War Thunder Setup Read README.md for full documentation

1. Example Sights & Auto Discovery in sights/

The sights/ folder includes ready to use example sights and acts as a live library. Drop any .blk file into sights/ and run Start-ReticleStudio.bat (or click Link sights/ in the top bar) to discover every sight in the dropdown.

2. Drawing Over Existing Sights

Select any sight from sights/ or click Overlay .blk. Classic preset files synthesize their static crosshair and range scale into the Base Layer. Lock the Base Layer and draw your custom artwork on the Drawn Art Overlay layer.

3. Reticles vs. Tank Sight HUD Presets (Two File Architecture)

War Thunder separates custom sights into two distinct .blk files in two separate folders (HUD settings placed inside all_tanks or <tank-ID> sight files are ignored by the game):
• <filename>_reticle.blk: Contains only the drawn reticle geometry (drawLines, drawQuads, drawCircles, drawTexts, crosshair_distances, matchExpClass) and goes into UserSights\all_tanks\ or UserSights\<tank-ID>\.
• <filename>_preset.blk: Contains the 1920x1080 HUD widgets (rangefinder, turret, fov, gunReady, bulletType), 4 mode optic colors (Day, Illum, NVG, Thermal), reticle thickness and font overrides, and crosshair:t="<filename>_reticle" (linking it automatically to your reticle file), and goes into UserSights\tank_sight_presets\.
Clicking the primary Save .blk button in the top right corner (or pressing Ctrl + S) automatically exports both companion files whenever both reticle geometry and HUD preset settings are present. If only reticle shapes exist, it automatically exports only _reticle.blk; if only HUD widgets exist, it exports only _preset.blk. Dedicated export buttons also remain available in Tank Sight Preset (HUD).

4. Auto Drawing Images & Importing .svg Art

Click Ref Image (or drag and drop a PNG, JPG, or WebP onto the canvas), or click Import .svg to convert crisp SVG vector paths and <text> or <foreignObject> labels directly without rasterizing. In Ref Image & Pro Auto-Draw, choose Smart Line Art (Pure Centerline) for junction clean single strokes across crossings, or Line Art + Solid Fills (Smart Hybrid) to fill thick eyes and shadows as true hole preserving drawQuads alongside crisp strokes. Use Variable-Width Ink for tapered calligraphic ribbons, toggle Crop Trace Area to isolate part of an image, and switch Preview to Ink Mask + Vectors to fine tune Sensitivity and Spur Trim before clicking Auto-Draw Image → Reticle.

5. Optimizing Sight Complexity

Use the Detail Kept (5% to 99%) slider in Sight Optimizer & Complexity to smoothly reduce line and quad counts while staying inside War Thunder engine limits. Click Undo Optimize anytime to restore full detail.

6. Circular Rangefinders (TZF Style Radial Dials) & Range Preview

Open Circular Rangefinder (Radial) in the right sidebar to build native War Thunder radial scales (ballistics { bullet { radial:b=yes } } or top level radial:b=yes), or assign per element moveRadial:b=yes under Selection & CAD. Click Range Preview in the top bar to scrub or animate 0 to 4000 m zeroing, optic zoom, and rangefinder or reload HUD simulation.

7. Installing Your Files in War Thunder & Troubleshooting

When saving both files (ensure filenames have no spaces):
1. Place <filename>_reticle.blk inside:
  • All ground vehicles: C:\Users\<username>\Documents\My Games\WarThunder\Saves\<player-ID>\production\UserSights\all_tanks\<filename>_reticle.blk
  • Or specific vehicle: C:\Users\<username>\Documents\My Games\WarThunder\Saves\<player-ID>\production\UserSights\<tank-ID>\<filename>_reticle.blk
2. Place <filename>_preset.blk inside:
  • HUD Presets folder: C:\Users\<username>\Documents\My Games\WarThunder\Saves\<player-ID>\production\UserSights\tank_sight_presets\<filename>_preset.blk
3. In War Thunder (Options → Common Battle Settings → Sight settings), select <filename>_preset under Choose preset (which automatically loads <filename>_reticle under Reticle).
Troubleshooting: Alt + F9 to reload sights in game most probably will not work (a known War Thunder limitation). Restart the game after adding or editing a sight, and ensure filenames contain no spaces.
